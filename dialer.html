How the tags work: lead strength is scored from real Google signals — access complaints in reviews, call volume, closed days, and whether it's solo-run vs a chain. Website / Booking aren't returned by the data source, so mark those yourself after a quick look. Your status, notes and checks save automatically on this device. Call windows are shown in your Dubai time and recalculated live from each clinic's real US time zone (handles US daylight saving automatically). Best = their 9 to 11 in the morning, Backup = their 2 to 4 in the afternoon; closed days are excluded. DSO means the office is owned by a dental support organization, not the dentist you'd be calling — a scaling decision there usually has to clear a regional or corporate layer first.
These are the corporate groups behind a lot of "DSO"-tagged leads in this list — sized, sourced, and kept here so you can size up a buyer before you dial. Not a target list on its own: cold-calling a DSO's corporate line rarely reaches anyone who can say yes. Use it to recognize a chain fast, and to decide whether a DSO-owned lead is worth a call at all.
